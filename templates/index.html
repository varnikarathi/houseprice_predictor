About the System

King County Automated Valuation Model (AVM)

CasaPredict estimates residential property sale prices in King County, Washington (encompassing Seattle, Bellevue, Redmond, Kirkland, and surrounding Eastside and South King County areas) based on 21,411 verified real estate transactions.

The model evaluates properties across five core dimensions: Area, Bedrooms & Bathrooms, Location (Zipcode & Distance to Seattle), Property Age, and Amenities & Quality. Every valuation is archived in MongoDB for persistence and auditability.

  • ✓ Ensemble Random Forest Regressor trained on $\log(1 + \text{price})$ with exponential inversion
  • ✓ Empirical test performance: $R^2 = 0.8724$, $\text{MAE} = \$67,108$, $\text{MAPE} = 12.26\%$
  • ✓ Automated persistence to MongoDB `properties` and `predictions` collections
